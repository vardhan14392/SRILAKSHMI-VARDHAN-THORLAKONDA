Carrying forward the sacred heritage of traditional Indian iconography, THORLAKONDA GURAVAIAH is a venerated master artist dedicated to the preservation and creation of authentic temple architecture and divine sculptures.

At Sree Guru Arts, temple design is approached as a sacred sadhana. Whether hand-carving deities from Krishna Shila (black stone), executing high-relief stuccowork for Rajagopurams, or illuminating sanctum walls with real 24-karat gold leaf, every stroke strictly observes traditional Agamas and Vastu Shastra iconometry.
